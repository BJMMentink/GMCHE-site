How to Register

In order to safeguard your personal information, we are choosing not to offer online registration on the website at this time.

Process of Registration for Fall Semester 2026

Registration begins Tuesday morning, August 4th at 8 am sharp.

Step 1: Request

To register for a class, please email Donna Warner: dwmagnificat@aol.com and let her know your first choice for class section (see Classes tab for class list and sections) and the number of children you wish to enroll.

It is recommended that you also state your second choice in the event that there are no longer available seats in the section of your first choice.

Donna will make note of the time that your email registration request lands in her inbox.

Step 2: Watch for an email response from Donna

If there IS space in the class, Donna will contact you via email and request class registration information. She will conditionally hold your seat(s) for the space of one hour while you compose an email providing all the registration information requested below.

Step 3: Send your email - Easy Peasy

Donna will make note of the time that your email registration information lands in her inbox. Classes will be filled on a first come, first served basis as I receive your initial request coupled with the information for registration.

Step 4: If Donna sends you an invoice

That means your child's spot in that class is reserved and she will see him or her in class on the first day! YAY! You may pay then by Venmo, mail a check, or bring your cash or check payment to me on the first day of class. Check tuition information on the Classes tab.

The "Not Yet" Waitlist

If the class or section you chose is full, Donna will ask if you'd like to choose another class or be placed on the waitlist. Then simply pray! If all classes fill, the Holy Spirit may help her find a way to open another class.
