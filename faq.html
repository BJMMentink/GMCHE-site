Illness Guidelines

  • Hand sanitizer is provided at the door, sink is available for handwashing
  • High touch areas in the studio are sanitized regularly
  • Student must be fever-free without the use of medication for 24 hours before attending class.

Atelier Guidelines (By registering you agree to)

  • Bring your student to class no more than 5 minutes early (final cleaning/prepping will be happening before that time and the door will be locked)
  • Please aim to be on time for class out of respect for all students so that we may begin prayer and work and utilize the time that God has provided us.
  • In very cold or rainy weather, if possible, text 262 365-7266 at pick up time when you arrive, and students will be sent out one at a time to avoid crowding at the door.
  • St. Joseph's Atelier sessions are structured with guided lessons as well as work time. If a student arrives late or leaves early for a class, they may have trouble catching up or finishing that day's project. This can be distressing to most students and disruptive to the class. Late arrivals can also hold the class back, resulting in a late dismissal. Please try to minimize late arrivals and early departures when possible.

Questions & Answers

Who can take classes?

Catholic children from absolute beginner (age 4) to advanced can come to the Atelier. There is a class for all ability levels. Everyone can grow their skills and love for art! Please adhere to the age requirement for the class your child is registering for. Age requirements are firm.

How long is each class?

For Little Lambs the session is 2 hours 15 minutes. The duration of time for Art Foundations and Skills, Advanced Art for Teens, and Sacred Art will now be 3.5 hours.

Why is the class session so long?

Three hours for a session has benefited my students because they have ample time to explore newly introduced concepts at their own speed, experiment working in the medium we are using, and then get into a creative zone or flow state. I have found that young students' bodies still need a break to move, snack, and socialize. Last year I introduced a short recess time mid-class or at an appropriate breaking point in the work. It was very good for the children and actually helped them work better. Unfortunately, recess time subtracted from flow-state time after recess. Also, cleanup time often didn't happen because children were rushing out the door. The new three-and-a-half-hour session will allow for three hours of solid instruction and work time. The added thirty minutes allows sufficient time for a 20-minute snack/recess mid-session and a 10-minute cleanup time at session's end.

What if we miss a class/workshop/camp day?

I dedicate class time and my attentive presence to guiding students who are already mid-project. Therefore, students who miss a session must complete a separate make-up class before joining a main project.

I want to give each child who comes to me for instruction my "best yes." It is unwise to pause a class midstream to teach an abbreviated lesson to a child who has missed. I have tried. No one benefits! Each child deserves to receive a proper foundation before jumping into a project and then, once begun to work, needs support readily available so he or she can get into a flow zone.

Please allow me to give you a snapshot of a typical class session to help you understand: There is a period of introduction and education before beginning a new project. I introduce a master artist and the style which characterizes his work. We observe several examples of his art. Students are engaged in analysis, they discuss what they notice and they learn from one another. We discuss how the master artist used elements of art or how she packaged ideas in the composition. When students are thus prepared, I introduce the medium we will use for the project. Students then explore materials' properties through mark-making experiments. Finally, I introduce the project and explain how the master artist can guide us. Students plan their composition and begin their work. Depending on the depth of the project, students may need another session to complete their work.

Fast forward to the next class session. I must get the ongoing students settled and working on their projects. Catching up a student requires dedicated, one-on-one instruction. Otherwise, the child who has missed the introduction phase may feel confused or lost, and this may affect their total experience.

Class days missed due to illness or emergency: I can offer to schedule a make-up class within 30 days of the missed class. I have set aside a couple of days each month for make-up classes should we need them. (See Calendar.) Again, these days are for those who miss for illness or emergency only. Because life is so unpredictable, there are no guarantees for a make-up class. There are only so many hours in a day, week, or month, and planning a make-up can get tricky. As always, I will try to accommodate a child/family as much as possible. I understand that a homeschooling mom's days are absolutely packed and no one can predict illness or emergencies. I so admire you and will try to work with you as much as my schedule will allow.

When your child is sick, please call or text to let us know not to expect your student so that we don't wait to start class.

We may take a vacation; can we skip classes?

This summer of 2026, I have been working carefully to structure my curriculum so that each project builds sequentially for the benefit of student development.

Please plan to have your child at all scheduled classes. If you know in advance that there will be three or more missed days due to a planned family vacation, sports, or another class or activity, you may risk interrupting the learning momentum at the Atelier for your child. There are only 8 sessions and 21 seats available for the Art Fundamentals and Skills. There are only 4 sessions and 14 seats available for Little Lambs. If you find that your schedule for this year is already very full with other enriching activities, and that your child will miss several classes, prayerfully consider enrolling your child another year and save spots in class for those students who can benefit from consistent engagement and participation.

All that being said, I honor and reverence family together time, and I will work with you to plan one make-up day in advance of your trip so your child can rejoin the art adventures when you return. Please contact me the week before you leave to give me time to consider how things could work. Due to time constraints, as I myself will be a student taking classes with Integrated Catholic, I may need to charge an additional fee of $10.00 per student to work in a make-up class before or after your vacation.

Please note: Art Café Days cannot be used for a make-up class because those days will be a completely different entity with different media set out and my attention will be required by attendees.

Can I wait until the spring to sign up? Or can I sign up only for the fall?

Classes at St. Joseph's Atelier are not random art experiences. I have worked on crafting and fine-tuning the curriculum to be cumulative and cyclical, meaning that there will be a variety of projects that introduce art concepts and then later projects that revisit content as students have developed a deeper command of the tools, confidence in techniques, and use of various art media. As the year progresses, students develop greater presence or focus and a sharpened self-awareness and presence as they work. The projects I will introduce in spring will incorporate skills and practice for concepts introduced in the autumn projects. Skipping the fall is not ideal, but I will discuss this on a case-by-case basis.

Can I sign up only for the fall semester?

I want to be open to families who request this; however, when your children leave after the fall, they will indeed leave a seat(s) open. Those seats can only be filled by someone who is familiar with the concepts introduced in the fall and is capable of joining midstream. I will ask you to pray for me that it all works out and I am able to fill those vacated seats if you find that you must do this.

What if the class/workshop/camp is cancelled?

If the instructor has to cancel a class due to illness or in the event of inclement weather, you will be refunded or given credit.

Can I meet the instructor and visit the studio before registering?

Absolutely! Just make an appointment, as the Atelier is at my private residence. Appointments can be made on teaching days before or after class time. No weekend appointments.

Can I stay to watch?

For the comfort of all of the students and because of space limitations, all classes are drop-off only. When parents linger, many students, including those that aren't yours, become self-conscious. If your child does not do well in a drop-off setting, it may be best to wait until they are older. If your child requires an assistant for special needs, please discuss in advance of registration.

Is there a place for parents (and younger children) to go during classes?

If you have a nursing baby and it's just you and you need a quiet place to sit and read, you may use our private living area upstairs. If you want to meet with friends, there is an excellent coffee shop, Coffeeville, right in Jackson. If you have younger siblings with you, there is a neighborhood park and walking trails in Jackson for when it is fair weather. Slinger or West Bend libraries are within a 15-minute drive. Cedarburg and Grafton are down the road going east on Highway 60.

My child doesn't meet the age requirement, but they are advanced. Can I sign up anyway?

No. There is a set age requirement for a multitude of reasons, including social and emotional readiness, just like there is for school or other activities. To be fair to all students and families that have been waiting to join until the required age, we cannot make any exceptions.
