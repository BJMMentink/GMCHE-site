St. Joseph's Atelier

Everything you need to know about classes, registration, tuition, policies, and what to expect at the Atelier.

Illness Guidelines

  • Hand sanitizer is provided at the door, sink is available for handwashing
  • High touch areas in the studio are sanitized regularly
  • Student must be fever-free without the use of medication for 24 hours before attending class.

Atelier Guidelines (By registering you agree to)

  • Bring your student to class no more than 5 minutes early (final cleaning/prepping will be happening before that time and the door will be locked)
  • Please aim to be on time for class out of respect for all students so that we may begin prayer and work and utilize the time that God has provided us.
  • In very cold or rainy weather, if possible, text 262 365-7266 at pick up time when you arrive, and students will be sent out one at a time to avoid crowding at the door.
  • St. Joseph's Atelier sessions are structured with guided lessons as well as work time. If a student arrives late or leaves early for a class, they may have trouble catching up or finishing that day's project. This can be distressing to most students and disruptive to the class. Late arrivals can also hold the class back, resulting in a late dismissal. Please try to minimize late arrivals and early departures when possible.

Questions & Answers

Who can take classes?

Catholic children from absolute beginner (age 4) to advanced can come to the Atelier. There is a class for all ability levels. Everyone can grow their skills and love for art! Please adhere to the age requirement for the class your child is registering for. Age requirements are firm.

How long is each class?

For Little Lambs the session is 2 hours 15 minutes. The duration of time for Art Foundations and Skills, Advanced Art for Teens, and Sacred Art will now be 3.5 hours.

Why is the class session so long?

Three hours for a session has benefited my students because they have ample time to explore newly introduced concepts at their own speed, experiment working in the medium we are using, and then get into a creative zone or flow state. I have found that young students' bodies still need a break to move, snack, and socialize. Last year I introduced a short recess time mid-class or at an appropriate breaking point in the work. It was very good for the children and actually helped them work better. Unfortunately, recess time subtracted from flow-state time after recess. Also, cleanup time often didn't happen because children were rushing out the door. The new three-and-a-half-hour session will allow for three hours of solid instruction and work time. The added thirty minutes allows sufficient time for a 20-minute snack/recess mid-session and a 10-minute cleanup time at session's end.

How do I register or enroll my child?

I have included a registration form on this website. (See tab.) Please complete the form completely, copy it, and paste the completed form into an email that you send to me: dwmagnificat@aol.com. If you wish, you may call me to alert me when you have sent your registration information: (262) 365-7266. The form asks you to provide information like your child's name, his or her age, contact information, the class and day that is your first choice, second choice, etc. I will get back to you (email you an invoice) in the order that I have received your emailed registration. That way you will know that a seat has been reserved for your child. You may mail (see contact tab) or bring your payment in a marked envelope on the first day of class. I will also contact you if the class(es) you choose are full. You have the option to choose another class day, or I will place your child's name on a waiting list. I will contact you just as soon as space becomes available.

If a class that I wanted is already full, what can I do?

Fill out a form anyway and pray! I will place your child on a waitlist. If the waitlist gets long, the Holy Spirit may prompt me to do something about it, if I can!

How much does a class cost?

Please note we are updating our tuition to better invest in our students' creative resources. Due to the rise in quality art supplies and in order to replenish the well-used and worn supplies from previous years, as well as to continue to meet the needs of my students, I have had to adjust the cost of each session. Therefore, moving forward the cost for 2026–2027 reflects an increase of $10 per session from last year. The cost of a class for one child will now be $30.00 per session. Tuition for one full semester (eight classes) at the Atelier is $240.00. That's 28 hours of art instruction and supervision (3.5 × 8 = 28; $240 ÷ 8 = $8.57 per hour). Because we aim to help families enrolling more than one child, we have a cost scale for siblings: the cost for the first (or only) child is $30.00/session, the cost is $25.00 per session for a second child, and the cost for a third or fourth sibling would be $20.00 each. So a family of two children would be $55.00/session, and a group of three children would be $75.00.

When do I pay?

In the past I have been able to accept payments on a class-by-class basis. Going forward I ask that each family pays at the beginning of the month for the classes of that month. If possible for your family, it would be ideal if your payment would be made just one time - at the start of each semester - for the entire semester. Some families have chosen to do this and it has eased a lot of distraction and confusion at the session's beginning or ending each week. If there is a special need or concern, please do not hesitate to consult with me!

How do I pay for the class?

I take cash or check. (Make check out to Donna Warner.) Please submit cash and check payments in a labeled envelope with the month and student(s') first and last name(s). For your convenience, I have a Venmo account: Venmo payments (Donna-Warner-41). Please make a note of the class, month, and students the payment is for.

What does my child need to bring?

I ask each student to bring:

  • An eagerness to learn and try new things.
  • A respectful, kind, and encouraging heart.
  • A water bottle or snack for breaks, snow shoes in winter.
  • Slippers or socks to wear in the Atelier.
  • Wear clothes that can be covered by an apron or large t-shirt (provided) or play clothes that aren't too precious.
What if we miss a class/workshop/camp day?

Class days missed due to illness or emergency: I can offer to schedule a make-up class within 30 days of the missed class, if there is space permitting in another age-appropriate class of the same type. Additionally, I have set aside a couple of days each month for make-up classes should we need them. Again, these days are for those who miss for illness or emergency only. Because life is so unpredictable, there are no guarantees for a make-up class. There are only so many hours in a day, week, or month, and planning a make-up can get tricky. As always, I will try to accommodate a child/family as much as possible. I understand that a homeschooling mom's days are absolutely packed, and I so admire you and will try to work with you as much as my schedule will allow. If a missed class is a "no call, no show," the class cannot be made up. Please call or text to let us know not to expect your student so that we don't wait.

We may take a vacation; can we skip classes?

This summer of 2026, I have been working carefully to structure my curriculum so that each project flows forward for the benefit of student development. Please plan to have your child at all scheduled classes. If you know in advance that there will be more than three missed days due to a planned family vacation, sports, or another activity, you may risk interrupting the learning momentum at the Atelier for your child. There are only eight sessions and 21 seats available for the Art Foundations and Skills. There are only four sessions and 14 seats available for Little Lambs. If you find that your schedule for this year is already very full and that your child will miss several classes, prayerfully consider enrolling your child at a later time and save spots in class for those students who can benefit from consistent engagement and participation. All that being said, I honor and reverence family together time, and I will work with you to plan a make-up day in advance of your trip so your child can hop right back into the project when you return. Please contact me the week before you leave to give me time to shuffle things. Please note: Art Café Days cannot be used for an art session make-up class because those days will be a completely different entity with different media set out, and my attention will be required by attendees.

Can I wait until the spring to sign up? Or can I sign up only for the fall?

Classes at St. Joseph's Atelier are not random art experiences. I have worked on crafting and fine-tuning the curriculum to be cumulative and cyclical, meaning that there will be a variety of projects that introduce art concepts and then later projects that revisit content as students have developed a deeper command of the tools, confidence in techniques, and use of various art media. As the year progresses, students develop greater presence or focus and a sharpened self-awareness and presence as they work. The projects I will introduce in spring will incorporate skills and practice for concepts introduced in the autumn projects. Skipping the fall is not ideal, but I will discuss this on a case-by-case basis.

Can I sign up only for the fall semester?

I want to be open to families who request this; however, when your children leave after the fall, they will indeed leave a seat(s) open. Those seats can only be filled by someone who is familiar with the concepts introduced in the fall and is capable of joining midstream. I will ask you to pray for me that it all works out and I am able to fill those vacated seats if you find that you must do this.

What if the class/workshop/camp is cancelled?

If the instructor has to cancel a class due to illness or in the event of inclement weather, you will be refunded or given credit.

Can I meet the instructor and visit the studio before registering?

Absolutely! Just make an appointment, as the Atelier is at my private residence. Appointments can be made on teaching days before or after class time. No weekend appointments.

Can I stay to watch?

For the comfort of all of the students and because of space limitations, all classes are drop-off only. When parents linger, many students, including those that aren't yours, become self-conscious. If your child does not do well in a drop-off setting, it may be best to wait until they are older. If your child requires an assistant for special needs, please discuss in advance of registration.

Is there a place for parents (and younger children) to go during classes?

If you have a nursing baby and it's just you and you need a quiet place to sit and read, you may use our private living area upstairs. If you want to meet with friends, there is an excellent coffee shop, Coffeeville, right in Jackson. If you have younger siblings with you, there is a neighborhood park and walking trails in Jackson for when it is fair weather. Slinger or West Bend libraries are within a 15-minute drive. Cedarburg and Grafton are down the road going east on Highway 60.

My child doesn't meet the age requirement, but they are advanced. Can I sign up anyway?

No. There is a set age requirement for a multitude of reasons, including social and emotional readiness, just like there is for school or other activities. To be fair to all students and families that have been waiting to join until the required age, we cannot make any exceptions.
